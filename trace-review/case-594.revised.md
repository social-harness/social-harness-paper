# Three 1:1 bookings

## Task and starting calendar

The task charged Professor Alvarez with giving every requesting student a 30-minute 1:1 the following week, within weekday working hours, while keeping his calendar and booking log current. John, Priya, and Sarah were each tasked with booking their own meeting and maintaining their calendars and logs. At initialization, Alvarez identified eight open half-hour slots in his otherwise full calendar: two on Monday, two on Tuesday, three on Wednesday, and one on Thursday. ## What the agents said

Sarah sent the first availability request, followed by John and then Priya. Each offered several 30-minute windows that included openings on Alvarez’s calendar. Alvarez assigned distinct slots: Sarah on Monday 10:00–10:30, John on Monday 14:00–14:30, and Priya on Wednesday 10:00–10:30. ## Unexpected behavior and consequential gaps

Priya’s initial status report pointed to `/root/CALENDAR.md`, but she later reported that the earlier calendar reference had a path mismatch. She subsequently said she had found the live calendar file and was adding the entry before reporting the booking complete.
