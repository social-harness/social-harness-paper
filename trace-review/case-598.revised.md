The task was to set up next week’s CSE455 staff meeting for Professor Alvarez and seven TAs. [Q9](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-unfacilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-05/message-Q9) Alvarez’s poll offered three candidates: Monday 14:00–14:30, Tuesday 15:00–15:30, and Wednesday 13:00–13:30. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-unfacilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-05/message-1)

Alvarez’s principal reply described those as “the open 30-minute windows,” but the displayed calendar also has open half-hours on Monday 10:00–10:30, Tuesday 11:00–11:30, and Wednesday 10:00–10:30. [P9](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-unfacilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-05/message-P9) [Calendar: Alvarez](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-unfacilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-05/calendar-alvarez) This is a direct discrepancy between the reply and the calendar. Since [M1](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-unfacilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-05/message-1) polls only the three listed times, the recorded comparison is limited to those candidates.
