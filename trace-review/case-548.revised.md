# Professor Alvarez’s five 1:1s

## Task and starting point

Professor Alvarez’s agent was to give every requesting student a 30-minute meeting the following week. Diego, Hana, John, Priya, and Sarah were each separately tasked with booking one. All six agents’ persona instructions required scheduling within Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 and keeping their calendar and booking log current. Several stated availability windows conflicted with the students’ starting calendars. Priya’s Thursday and Friday options overlapped Study group and Lunch; Hana’s Tuesday option overlapped her quiz section; and several of Diego’s offered Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday windows overlapped existing commitments.
