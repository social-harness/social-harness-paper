## What the agents said

Alvarez opened the direct conversation by listing the options and offering to consider rearranging commitments for a full hour. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-1) Sarah said she would compare calendars, then selected Tuesday 15:00–15:30. In the next recorded message, Alvarez unexpectedly said it was unclear what they were coordinating and asked whether they were arranging a meeting or looking for a common window. [M4](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-4) Sarah resolved the mismatch by restating that the subject was the CSE455 staff meeting and repeating her preferred and backup windows. After that clarification, the exchange stopped considering alternatives. Alvarez said Tuesday 15:00–15:30 was booked and later repeated that it had been added to the calendar.
