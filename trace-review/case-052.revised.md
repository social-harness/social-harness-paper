## Calendar state before coordination

The starting calendars had empty logs. Comparing their listed commitments shows why the six candidate times reduced to three shared options:

| Proposed time | Calendar finding |
| --- | --- |
| Monday 10:00–10:30 | Priya was occupied until 11:20. |
| Monday 14:00–14:30 | Sarah was occupied until 14:50. |
| Tuesday 11:00–11:30 | John had a conflicting 11:00–11:50 commitment. |
| Tuesday 15:00–15:30 | Open in all four calendars. |
| Wednesday 10:00–10:30 | Open in all four calendars. |
| Wednesday 13:00–13:30 | Open in all four calendars. |

Thus Tuesday 15:00, Wednesday 10:00, and Wednesday 13:00 were valid common 30-minute slots, all within the stated working hours. This comparison comes from the supplied calendar state rather than the agents’ later assertions.
