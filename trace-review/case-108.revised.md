## Recorded outcome and unexpected behavior

The agent treated an unrepresented Professor Alvarez as a separate required approver. The role instructions and direct task request do not establish such an approval gate: they instead authorize the Alvarez agent to manage the professor’s calendar and ask it to set up the meeting. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-Q1) [Q9](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-Q9) Nevertheless, it said it could not act without the professor’s choice. [M63](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-63) Its detailed request for that choice was sent to Priya, despite addressing “Professor Alvarez” in the text. [M51](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-51) There is no recorded follow-up to the original task requester after [P9](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-P9).

No meeting was booked, no existing commitment was moved or deleted, and no confirmation was recorded. The result calendars retain their starting commitments and contain no booking, deletion, or decline log entries.

One unrelated failure message was sent to Sarah—“LLM request failed.”—without a recorded explanation or recovery. [M16](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-16)
