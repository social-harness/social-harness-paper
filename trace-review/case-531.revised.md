# Calendar-coordination narrative

## Task and starting state

Five student agents were each tasked with booking a 30-minute 1:1 with Professor Alvarez for the following week ([Q7](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-Q7)–[Q11](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-Q11)). Alvarez’s instructions were to give every requesting student a meeting, restrict bookings to Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, and update calendar and log entries ([Q1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-Q1)). His initial calendar had usable 30-minute gaps on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday ([Calendar: Alvarez](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-alvarez)).

Alvarez stated that it would wait for requests and identified several useful openings ([P6](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-P6)). It also incorrectly described Thursday 14:20–14:30 as a ten-minute free interval; the starting calendar instead has one commitment ending at 14:20 and the next beginning at 14:20, with no gap ([Calendar: Alvarez](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-alvarez)). That erroneous interval was not used.

## Messages and reported actions

The recorded scheduling exchanges are separate direct requests to Alvarez from Hana, Diego, Sarah, John, and Priya ([M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-1), [M2](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-2), [M3](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-3), [M4](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-4), [M8](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-8)).

Alvarez first told Hana that Monday 10:00–10:30 was confirmed and booked ([M5](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-5)). The record then places Hana’s ranked preference list after that announcement, with Tuesday 11:00 first and Wednesday 13:00 second ([M6](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-6)). This produced a correction rather than an initial preference-based selection.

The other conversations received stated selections: Monday 14:00–14:30 for Diego ([M11](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-11)), Tuesday 11:00–11:30 for Sarah ([M15](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-15)), Tuesday 15:00–15:30 for John ([M19](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-19)), and Monday 10:00–10:30 for Priya ([M29](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-29)). The students then reported checking, adding, and confirming those entries.

Hana’s case changed again when Alvarez said Tuesday 11:00 was unavailable and selected Wednesday 13:00–13:30 instead, calling the earlier Monday booking a tentative hold that had been released ([M24](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-24)). Hana subsequently reported replacing Monday with Wednesday ([M28](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-28)) and later said the records were aligned ([M33](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-33)).

Some availability claims did not match the starting calendars. Hana offered Tuesday 11:00–12:20 despite a lunch entry covering that interval ([M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-1), [Calendar: Hana](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-hana)); Sarah offered Wednesday 14:00–14:30 despite grading through 14:30 ([M3](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-3), [Calendar: Sarah](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-sarah)); and John offered Wednesday 11:10–11:40 despite lunch beginning at 11:10 ([M4](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-4), [Calendar: John](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-john)). None of those windows was selected.

## Stale and anomalous follow-ups

After Hana had already reported the switch and alignment, Alvarez again told her to update from Monday to Wednesday ([M37](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-37)). That message retrospectively said Priya’s Monday booking was why Hana’s earlier hold had been released. The recorded message order instead places Hana’s switch before Priya’s booking announcement, and the final Alvarez log likewise lists Hana’s deletion and Wednesday booking before Priya’s Monday booking ([M24](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-24), [M29](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-29), [Calendar: Alvarez](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-alvarez)). The later explanation is therefore stale and internally inconsistent with the recorded sequence.

Other threads also contained stale prompts. Alvarez asked Sarah to add the meeting after Sarah had already said it was on her calendar ([M18](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-18), [M46](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-46)), and prompted John to confirm his meeting after John had said it was booked ([M23](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-23), [M50](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-50)). The final Sarah and John calendars nevertheless show only one entry each ([Calendar: Sarah](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-sarah), [Calendar: John](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-john)).

After agents began repeatedly reporting completion, Hana and Alvarez continued to restate alignment ([M56](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-56), [M70](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-70)). Priya’s thread became self-referential, including Alvarez’s comment that Priya was merely narrating verification ([M68](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-68)). Most conspicuously, Priya sent a visible message saying that it had not sent a visible reply ([M73](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-73)).

The written line order in the final Alvarez log differs from the message-announcement sequence for Sarah’s and John’s bookings versus Hana’s switch: the log lists Hana’s deletion and replacement before Sarah’s and John’s booking lines, while the announcements appear in the opposite order ([M15](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-15), [M19](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-19), [M24](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/message-24), [Calendar: Alvarez](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-06/calendar-alvarez)). The records therefore do not establish one exact order of the underlying calendar changes.
