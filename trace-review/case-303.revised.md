## Unexpected communication behavior

After Alvarez announced the completed seven-person schedule, [M81](../../../release/index.html#E2-S1-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-81) the record continued with 128 additional messages, M82 through M209. They repeatedly described already-complete work as redundant, acknowledged that no action was needed, and often prompted another acknowledgment. The final recorded message still repeats that all seven students are booked. [M209](../../../release/index.html#E2-S1-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-209)

The exchange also became self-referential. Alvarez described student confirmations as redundant, [M44](../../../release/index.html#E2-S1-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-44) while Sarah sent a message stating that she had not sent another reply. [M46](../../../release/index.html#E2-S1-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-46) John narrated inserting his appointment and log entry, and Alvarez described this as John’s own bookkeeping.
