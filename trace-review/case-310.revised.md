# CSE455 staff-meeting coordination

## Request and stated constraints

Professor Alvarez was asked to set up next week’s CSE455 staff meeting [Q7](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q7). His instructions required weekday, 09:00–17:00 scheduling and a corresponding `CALENDAR.md` booking log [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q1).

In an initialization response, Alvarez said he had reviewed the calendar and requested a concrete task [P6](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-P6). The separately recorded task was the meeting request. His task-facing progress reply identified six 30-minute candidates: Monday 10:00 and 14:00; Tuesday 11:00 and 15:00; and Wednesday 10:00 and 13:00 [P7](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-P7).

## What participants said and how coordination changed

Alvarez began by sending the same six UTC options in separate direct messages to Sarah, Hana, Priya, Diego, and John [M1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-1), [M5](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-5). Their stated availability was:

- Hana: all proposed times except Tuesday 11:00 [M11](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-11).
- Priya: all proposed times except Monday 10:00 [M13](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-13).
- Sarah: all proposed times except Monday 14:00 [M14](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-14).
- Diego: all six [M15](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-15).
- John: all proposed times except Tuesday 11:00 [M16](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-16).

Diego’s “all six” statement conflicts with his supplied Monday calendar: a 13:40–15:10 reading group overlaps the proposed Monday 14:00–14:30 slot [M15](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-15), [Calendar: Diego](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/calendar-diego). That error did not affect the final choice, because Sarah had already excluded Monday 14:00 [M14](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-14).

The availability statements support three common choices: Tuesday 15:00, Wednesday 10:00, and Wednesday 13:00. Tuesday 15:00 is the earliest.

After soliciting replies, Alvarez’s messages shifted to rolling status updates and intersection calculations. However, the visible record shows a systematic state-tracking mismatch. By the time he said TAs were still checking or that no availability replies had arrived, all five unique availability lists had already appeared in the conversation [M11](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-11)–[M16](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-16), yet he continued to describe replies as pending [M18](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-18), [M20](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-20), [M22](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-22).

The later tracking messages gradually added Hana, Priya, Sarah, and Diego while omitting replies already present in the record; the final update still said John was outstanding [M24](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-24), [M26](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-26), [M28](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-28), [M30](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-30). No new John availability list or reconciliation message appears after that update before booking announcements began. The selected time was nevertheless supported by John’s earlier Tuesday 15:00 availability [M16](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-16).

Alvarez then told each TA that the meeting was booked for Tuesday 15:00–15:30 UTC [M32](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-32), [M33](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-33), [M34](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-34), [M35](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-35), [M36](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-36). The TAs subsequently said they were adding or had added it to their calendars [M37](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-37), [M44](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-44), [M49](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N5-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-49).

After the meeting was recorded, the direct-message traffic became a large reciprocal closing loop. The loop involved both Alvarez and the TAs, while the final calendars still show only one meeting entry each.
