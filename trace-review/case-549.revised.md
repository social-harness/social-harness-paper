## Inconsistencies and unexpected behavior

Some proposed “available” windows conflict with the supplied starting student calendars. Diego offered two Tuesday options entirely within a 10:20–11:20 focus block; Sarah offered Tuesday lunch-time options and Friday focus-block options; and Hana offered Wednesday 10:20–10:50 during a research-group meeting.
