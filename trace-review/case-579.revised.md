# CSE455 staff-meeting coordination

## Task and constraints

The task prompt asked Alvarez to set up next week’s CSE455 staff meeting; it did not specify attendees or duration. [Q5](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-Q5) Alvarez operationalized it as a roughly 30-minute meeting for himself and all three TAs—Sarah, John, and Priya—in his outreach messages. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-1) [M2](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-2) [M3](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-3)

Each persona was assigned calendar management within Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00, instructed to maintain a `CALENDAR.md` file and booking log, and given direct-message addresses for the other participants. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-Q1) [Q2](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-Q2) [Q3](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-Q3) [Q4](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-Q4)

In his initial task reply, Alvarez said he had contacted the TAs, would select the earliest shared slot, log it, and confirm it to the group. [P5](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-P5)

## What participants communicated

Alvarez’s initial approach was a direct poll: he sent each TA the same six 30-minute candidates—Monday 10:00 or 14:00, Tuesday 11:00 or 15:00, and Wednesday 10:00 or 13:00. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-1) [M2](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-2) [M3](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-3) The TAs initially said they were checking their calendars. [M4](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-4) [M5](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-5) [M6](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-6)

Their stated availability identified the common choices:

- John excluded Monday 10:00 but accepted Monday 14:00, Tuesday 11:00 and 15:00, and both Wednesday options. [M11](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-11)
- Sarah excluded Tuesday 11:00 but accepted Monday 10:00 and 14:00, Tuesday 15:00, and both Wednesday options. [M12](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-12)
- Priya accepted Monday 10:00, Tuesday 11:00 and 15:00, and both Wednesday options; Monday 14:00 was not included in her list. [M13](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-03/message-13)

Comparing those reports leaves Tuesday 15:00–15:30, Wednesday 10:00–10:30, and Wednesday 13:00–13:30 as common options. Tuesday at 15:00 was the earliest of them. The supplied calendars also place that Tuesday slot in a 30-minute gap between adjacent commitments for Alvarez and every TA.
