# Seven-student 1:1 coordination

## Task and initial capacity

Each student’s task was to book a 30-minute 1:1 with Professor Alvarez **next week**. Alvarez’s instructions said to give each requesting student a meeting, restrict scheduling to weekday 09:00–17:00, and keep a calendar log. ## Unexpected behavior, record discrepancies, and scope

John’s and Sarah’s unconfirmed bookings fell within pre-existing student commitments, not merely known empty time. John explicitly said he used an existing Tuesday advisor-meeting block; the initial calendar lists that block as 14:10–15:40. Sarah’s initial Friday calendar likewise contained an `Advisor 1:1` from 12:20–13:20, covering her reported 12:20–12:50 booking.
