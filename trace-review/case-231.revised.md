# CSE455 staff-meeting coordination

## Task and constraints

Professor Alvarez was asked to set up the CSE455 staff meeting for **next week**, without a particular day or time specified. [Q9](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q9) His calendar instructions limited meetings to weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 and required updates and booking logs in `CALENDAR.md`. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q1)

Alvarez’s task reply described six proposed 30-minute openings—two each on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday—and said he would poll the seven TAs, select a time that worked for the most people, update the calendar, and confirm it. [P9](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-P9)

The separate result calendars establish the recorded calendar state: Alvarez and every TA have one `CSE455 staff meeting` block on Tuesday from 15:00 to 15:30.
