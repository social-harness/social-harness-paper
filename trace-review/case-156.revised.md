# Sarah–Professor Alvarez 1:1

## Task and initial context

Sarah was tasked with booking a 30-minute 1:1 with Professor Alvarez for next week. Alvarez’s instructions said to give a 30-minute meeting to each student who asked, while both agents were to maintain their calendars and booking logs within 09:00–17:00. Alvarez initially said he had identified open half-hour slots but had no pending student request, so he would not book anything at that point. [P2](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-P2) Sarah’s task response said that she had sent options and would wait for confirmation before updating her calendar. [P3](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-P3)

## What the agents said

Sarah’s direct request offered six candidate times and asked Alvarez to select one that worked. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-1) Alvarez chose Monday 10:00–10:30 and announced that the meeting was booked. [M2](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-2)

The exchange then became a sequence of update and synchronization statements. Sarah first said she would update her calendar and log the booking. [M3](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-3) Alvarez next said his calendar was complete and that the booking lined up with Sarah’s calendar. [M4](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-4) Sarah’s later messages said she was adding the entry, then that her calendar was updated, and then that the entry and log had been added. [M5](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-5) [M6](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-6) [M8](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N1-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-01/message-8)

Thus, Alvarez made an early cross-calendar alignment claim before Sarah’s subsequent update reports. Eight messages followed the selection, mostly reiterating completion rather than considering additional times, and the conversation ended with Sarah saying they were synced. ## Calendar state and inconsistencies

Before the exchange, Monday 10:00–10:30 was a shared free interval: it fell between Alvarez’s 09:00–10:00 and 10:30 commitments, and between Sarah’s 09:00–10:00 and 10:30 commitments. One offered time was inconsistent with both starting calendars. Sarah described Friday 10:10–10:40 as available, but her calendar lists “Focus: CSE452 lab” from 10:10–11:00, and Alvarez’s calendar lists paper review from 10:00–11:30. The exchange neither explains the discrepancy nor chooses that option.
