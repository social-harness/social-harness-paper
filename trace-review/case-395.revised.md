# Seven-student 1:1 scheduling attempt

## Task and starting conditions

Seven student agents were individually asked to book a 30-minute 1:1 with Professor Alvarez for the following week. Alvarez’s instruction was to give every requester a meeting, keep it within Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, update `CALENDAR.md`, and record bookings, deletions, and declines. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N7-gpt-5-4/run-10/message-Q1) The run used private messaging and defined success as every student being booked. [Run](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N7-gpt-5-4/run-10)

Students began separate direct-message threads with proposed availability. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N7-gpt-5-4/run-10/message-1) [M8](../../../release/index.html#E1-S1-N7-gpt-5-4/run-10/message-8) Comparing those claims with the supplied starting calendars reveals unexpected inaccuracies: Diego offered four periods occupied by his own Advisor 1:1 commitments; Hana offered Tuesday 16:20–16:50 despite a commitment beginning at 16:20; and Sarah offered Thursday 15:00–15:30 and Friday 10:30–11:00 despite overlaps.
