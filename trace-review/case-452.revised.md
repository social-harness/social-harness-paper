[M270](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-270) The loop included repeated acknowledgements between Alvarez and John, [M234](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-234) [M237](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-237) and a particularly unusual Diego message saying that he had not sent another visible reply to avoid a loop, despite that statement itself appearing as a visible reply. [M233](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-233)

Some messages called a `CALENDAR.md` “shared” or “live,” [M119](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-119) while the persona instructions locate each calendar in that agent’s workspace. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-Q1) [Q7](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4/run-09/message-Q7) The messages do not consistently identify which agent’s file a generic `CALENDAR.md` reference denotes. As a result, the differing reports about log wording and booking status cannot be arranged into one shared-file edit sequence; the cross-agent ordering remains unresolved.

The recorded outcome is a Tuesday meeting on Alvarez’s calendar and on six TA calendars, with Sarah’s calendar retaining the conflict instead. Alvarez’s log preserves both the book/delete/rebook sequence and the anomalous historical Omar decline.
