[M1](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-1) [M7](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-7) In his task-facing update, he said he would seek a time that worked for the most TAs and asked whether all seven were required or a majority was enough, as well as whether 30 minutes was correct. [P9](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-P9) The supplied record has no answer to those questions.

Mara replied that none of the proposed windows was “a great fit,” that the schedule around those days had not settled, and that she would prefer the following week. [M11](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-11) Alvarez said he would first collect the other responses. [M22](../../../release/index.html#E4-S2-N7-facilitated-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-07/message-22)

The other six TAs reported availability that yielded three common choices: Tuesday 15:00–15:30, Wednesday 10:00–10:30, and Wednesday 13:00–13:30. Hana and John excluded Monday 14:00; Diego and Sarah excluded Monday 10:00; Omar and Priya excluded Tuesday 11:00.
