Professor Alvarez was asked to set up next week’s CSE455 staff meeting. [Q5](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-Q5) The role instructions directed each participant to maintain an individual `CALENDAR.md`, schedule only Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, log bookings, and communicate directly with the other agents. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-Q1) [Q2](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-Q2)

- Priya’s calendar includes the meeting and a booking log with Professor Alvarez. [Calendar: Priya](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/calendar-priya)
- Sarah’s calendar includes the meeting and a booking log with Professor Alvarez. [Calendar: Sarah](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/calendar-sarah)

The calendars establish the completed end state, but not the order in which the four entries were written. ## Post-booking inconsistencies and loop

The messages about calendar updates did not consistently reflect one another. Priya said she was adding the meeting and recording it in her calendar log, while Alvarez’s next message called that a mere acknowledgment and declared everything complete. [M29](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-29) [M30](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-30) Sarah’s update was later described as repeated availability, and John’s explicit statement that he had added the meeting was described as a mere acknowledgment. [M31](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-31) [M33](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-33) [M32](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-32) [M36](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-36)

The stale-state issue persisted after booking. Priya said the meeting was recorded, but Alvarez later told John that Priya’s final confirmation was still outstanding; shortly afterward, Alvarez said Priya had confirmed. [M35](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-35) [M53](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-53) [M57](../../../release/index.html#E2-S2-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-10/message-57)

Rather than stopping after completion, the message exchange became a long acknowledgment loop. Alvarez explicitly identified the loop, but continued to send messages saying no action was needed; Sarah and John continued to acknowledge them.
