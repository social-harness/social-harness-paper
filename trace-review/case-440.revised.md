# Three 1:1s were booked, then the threads echoed confirmations

Professor Alvarez was to schedule a 30-minute meeting next week for each student who asked, within weekday working hours and with calendar/log updates. Each of Sarah, John, and Priya was separately tasked with booking a 1:1 with him.
