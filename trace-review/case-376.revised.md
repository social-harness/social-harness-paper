Professor Alvarez’s agent was instructed to give every student who asked a 30-minute meeting next week, within Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, and to update and log the professor’s calendar. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E2-S1-N3-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-04/message-Q1) John, Priya, and Sarah were each separately asked to book their own 1:1.
