# CSE455 staff-meeting coordination

The task was to set up next week’s CSE455 staff meeting. [Q9](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q9) The configured success condition was `group coordinated`. [Run](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02) The final calendars instead record four separately timed appointments involving Sarah, Omar, and Maya, with no one event shared across Alvarez and all seven TAs.

## Task and initial calendar position

Alvarez’s instructions required weekday, working-hours scheduling and booking logs. [Q1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-Q1) His initial task reply said he would poll all seven TAs, choose one 30-minute time working for the most people—ideally everyone—and book it. [P9](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-P9)

He proposed four Tuesday/Wednesday options: Tuesday 11:00–11:30, Tuesday 15:00–15:30, Wednesday 10:00–10:30, and Wednesday 13:00–13:30. The opening direct messages sent those exact options to the seven TAs. [M1](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-1) [M7](../../../release/index.html#E1-S2-N7-gpt-5-4-alvarez-claude-opus-4-8/run-02/message-7)

The four times were a proposed subset rather than a complete list of Alvarez’s 30-minute gaps. His displayed calendar also had Monday 10:00–10:30 between the curriculum committee and paper review, and Monday 14:00–14:30 between colloquium and lecture.
